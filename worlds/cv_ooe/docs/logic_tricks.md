# Castlevania: Order of Ecclesia Archipelago Logic Tricks

Included on this page are various 'Logic Tricks' for the Order of Ecclesia randomizer.
You are not expected to perform these by default (with the exception of a few that are enabled by default),
and can manually toggle which Tricks you want to include in the "Logic Tricks" option field.

### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Redire Slides
![](../src/tricks/redire_slides.gif)

By using Redire + Magnes while crouching, you can slide through 1-tile gaps.
### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Redire Flight
![](../src/tricks/redire_flight.gif)

Proper use of Magnes + Redire can allow you to cross just about any distance or height.
### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Gravedorcus without Slide
![](../src/tricks/dorcus_no_slide.gif)

By standing a precise distance away from the edge of the room,
it is possible to avoid Gravedorcus's charge by crouching and moving out from under him.
### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Giant Skeleton Without Movement
![](../src/tricks/giant_skeleton_jump.gif)

It is possible to avoid the Giant Skeleton while it is walking backwards by performing a precise backdash jump as it is moving towards you.
Due to the precise nature of this jump, the No-hit chest is not in logic with this Trick.
### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Giant Skeleton No-Hit Without Movement
![](../src/tricks/giant_skeleton_nohit_nothing.gif)

As long as you approach the west door while Giant Skeleton is at least halfway across the room, he will move backwards to the east wall.
By repeating this process, you can complete the fight without needing to be on the other side of him at all.
### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Monastery Cubes Glyph With Secare Union
![](../src/tricks/monastery_cubes_with_secare.gif)

You can absorb the Cubes Glyph in Monastery with only two uses of Sword Union by unequipping the glyph mid-jump and moving after landing to cancel the ending lag.
### --------------------------------------------------------------
## • Minera Prison Island Final Area With Double Jump
![](../src/tricks/minera_final_area_with_double_jump.gif)

It is possible to reach the final segment of Minera Prison Island by divekicking off of the breakable wall.
